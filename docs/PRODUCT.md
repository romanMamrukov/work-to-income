# Product scope

## Positioning

**Work-to-income control for Latvian freelancers.**

The first target segment is an independent service professional who sends a small number of invoices each month and needs a clear relationship between work delivered, money received, business costs, a tax reserve and spendable cash.

## Core journeys

### Direct invoice

Create invoice → select or add client → add line items → save draft → download PDF → mark sent → mark paid.

### Work to invoice

Add client → add work → optionally run timer → complete work → create invoice from work → mark paid.

### Money control

Record income and expenses → classify deductible expenses → calculate planning reserve → show safe-to-spend estimate.

## Deliberate exclusions

- Open Banking and payment initiation
- Email inbox access
- Incoming-document OCR
- Automatic bank reconciliation
- Tax filing or guaranteed tax calculations
- Team accounting and accountant portal

These exclusions keep the product distinct from SoloHub and reduce security, regulatory and operational risk during validation.

## Validation targets

- 15 interviews with Latvian freelancers or self-employed professionals
- 10 users completing the direct-invoice flow without assistance
- 7 of 10 users correctly understanding reserve versus safe-to-spend
- 3 users willing to pay or 2 paid setup pilots
- Tax-reserve methodology reviewed by a qualified Latvian accountant before a production tax claim
